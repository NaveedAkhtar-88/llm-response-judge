LLM Response Judge

A rubric for comparing two model answers to the same prompt. Score each answer on weighted criteria, flag hard problems, pick an overall preference and write the reason. The tool checks that your preference agrees with your scores, which is the most common slip in evaluation work. Nothing leaves your browser.
